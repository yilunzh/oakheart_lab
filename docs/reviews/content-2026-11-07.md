## Open for the owner (not changed)

2. **Named booking platforms** ("FareHarbor, Peek, Mindbody or Square") imply proven integrations and none fit the heat pump example. Proposed: "No. We work with the booking system you already have, and connect to it where it allows."
3. **"Also available: a companion mobile app, and staff tools and automation."** Companion to what, for whom, and is it inside the fee?
