# Messaging hierarchy (Phase 2)

Sources: `docs/brief.md` (facts and constraints) and `docs/research/phase1-market.md` (evidence; section numbers below refer to it).

## Positioning in one line
Oakheart Lab makes sure AI assistants find your business, describe it correctly, and send customers into a booking flow that works, for businesses that move atoms, not bits.

## Why we win (research §4c)
1. **Accuracy, not just presence.** Competitor audits only check whether you're mentioned. We check what AI gets *wrong*: availability, age limits, what's included, deposits, cancellation. Those are the facts that break bookings.
2. **One team from found to booked, kept ahead every month.** Visibility tools stop at visibility. Booking agencies stop at the website.
3. **Keep your booking system.** We work with FareHarbor, Peek, Checkfront, Mindbody, Vagaro, Square, Housecall Pro and similar.
4. **Built for operations-heavy businesses:** capacity, time slots, assets, staff.
5. **An operator's product leader.** A decade building commerce where "you're ultimately moving atoms, not bits." Roles only, no employer metrics.
6. **Honest measurement:** mention rate and factual errors across repeated runs. No fake "AI rank."
7. **Facts, not content volume.** We don't sell a monthly article quota. For booking businesses, a handful of operational facts decide what AI says. See `docs/research/competitor-firstindex.md`.

## Section messages
1. **The shift (evidence, at most 3 stats, each sourced and dated):**
   - Pew: when Google showed an AI summary, people clicked a result 8% of the time, against 15% without one.
   - BrightLocal: 45% of consumers used AI tools for local recommendations.
   - Google I/O 2026: Search will book local experiences and services for people.
   - Takeaway: the answer is becoming the storefront.
2. **Recognize yourself (questions customers actually ask AI):**
   - "My furnace is 20 years old. Should I repair it or switch to a heat pump?" (home services)
   - "Can my 6-year-old do the kayak tour?" (experiences)
   - "Does the boat rental include fuel?" (rentals)
   - "Which lakeside cabins near Asheville take two big dogs, and what's the pet fee?" (stays)
   - "I cracked a tooth. Which dentist near me takes my insurance and can see me tomorrow?" (clinics)
3. **Three ways it costs you:** AI doesn't mention you; AI mentions you with wrong details; the customer clicks through and the booking flow loses them. Marketplaces then charge 20–30% commission for demand you could have taken directly (category ranges, sourced).
4. **The system:**
   - **Found:** be the business AI can find, understand and cite.
   - **Booked:** turn that visit into a confirmed booking, with the right add-ons.
   - **Stay ahead:** every month, re-check against competitors, adapt to new AI features, keep facts current. (Not a support bot; owner 2026-10-11.)
5. **Founder:** "I've spent over a decade building commerce where the click turns into a car, a key, a delivery. Now I help businesses like yours do the same with AI." Roles: Hertz, Rivian, Carvana, Clutch.
6. **How it works:** free check (<24h) → a plan of specific fixes across Discover and Book, (shown as a labeled sample plan) → monthly ongoing work, all under one flat monthly fee: re-run the check against competitors, keep facts current, adapt to new AI features. Shown as a loop, not an add-on.
7. **Risk reversal (exact):** "If you're not happy with our service, we'll give your money back, no questions asked."
8. **FAQ:**
   - Do I have to switch booking systems? (No)
   - Isn't this just SEO? (Mostly good SEO, plus the facts and booking path AI depends on)
   - Can you guarantee ChatGPT recommends us? (No; here's what we can do)
   - What does it cost? (One flat monthly fee covering fixes and upkeep, month to month, plus the money-back promise)
   - How does the check work?
   - What do you need from me?
