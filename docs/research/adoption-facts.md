## Companies acting fast on AI assistants as a booking and local-discovery channel

1. **Booking.com and Expedia in ChatGPT (2025-10-06).** They were launch partners for apps in ChatGPT at DevDay 2025, alongside Spotify, Zillow, Canva, Figma and Coursera. OpenAI said DoorDash, Instacart, Uber and AllTrails would follow.
   - https://openai.com/index/introducing-apps-in-chatgpt/
   - https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/06/openai-launches-apps-inside-of-chatgpt/
2. **FareHarbor in ChatGPT (2026-04-15).** "Travelers can now discover and book tours and activities from FareHarbor operators within ChatGPT," with real-time availability and pricing. Source: FareHarbor's own blog (primary).
   - https://marketing.fareharbor.com/blog/chatgpt-fareharbors-newest-distribution-partner/
3. **Google agentic booking for local services (2026-05-19, I/O 2026).** Google is "expanding agentic booking capabilities in Search to a wide range of new tasks, including local experiences and services." For home repair, beauty and pet care, Google can call businesses on the user's behalf. The rollout was to everyone in the U.S. in summer 2026.
   - https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/
4. **Yelp licenses data to OpenAI (2026-07-23, Axios exclusive).** Yelp reviews, photos and business info appear in ChatGPT answers with Yelp branding and links. Yelp's "Request a Quote" is coming to ChatGPT. CEO Jeremy Stoppelman said: "If you want to answer local queries, you really need Yelp."
   - https://www.axios.com/2026/07/23/yelp-reviews-chatgpt-geo-partnership

Not checked: OpenTable and Tripadvisor apps in ChatGPT. I did not look for these.
