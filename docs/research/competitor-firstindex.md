# Competitor note: FirstIndex (firstindex.co)

Checked 2026-10-10. Read from the public homepage only. Figures are as FirstIndex states them, not verified by us.

## What they sell
- **Headline:** "Get found everywhere people ask." Subhead: "SEO and AI visibility for software companies, done for you."
- **Buyer:** software companies. Not our buyer: little overlap with operators who move atoms.
- **Mechanism:** "One real change ships to your site every day," each checked by a person, with a Monday report.
- **Price:** one plan, $2,000/month, set against "about $25,000/month" for a full agency. Includes up to 12 articles, 50+ page fixes, comparison pages, free tools, index checks, AI mention tracking with corrections, directory listings, link outreach and a weekly report.
- **Money back:** first month only, requested within 7 days after it ends.
- **Entry:** no free audit. "Start with one sentence" ("When someone asks ChatGPT… the answer should include…"), and a yes or an honest no within 1 business day. Text the founder.
- **Proof:** one chart of their own data (clicks per day over 90 days). No logos or testimonials.

## Where they are stronger
1. The paid work is easy to picture (a change a day, a Monday report).
2. The price is visible and anchored.
3. The first step makes the buyer state the outcome in AI terms.
4. Proof from their own data.

## Where we differ
1. **Accuracy of operational facts, not content volume.** For a booking business, what AI says about age limits, inclusions, availability and policies matters more than article count.
2. **Past the click.** They stop at "Mentioned." We continue into Book and Support, on the client's existing booking system.
3. **Value before commitment.** Free, unlimited check in under 24 hours.
4. **Broader risk reversal.** Ours covers all paid work with no window. We state the approved sentence on its own; we do not compare.
5. **Operator background.** A decade in automotive commerce fits our buyer.
